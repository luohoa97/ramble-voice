Ramble Voice 1.0.0, the first stable release.
- Natural dictation: rambling, pauses, restarts and noise understood; the model reasons about what you mean.
- Ramble keyboard and floating mic; numbers and passwords go to your typing keyboard.
- Lists, commands, Markdown, all languages in their own script.
- Field-aware text (search boxes, emails, chats) and optional conversation context.
- Long recordings stitched without losing words.
- On-device Gemma 4 or your own Gemini key. Android 12 and newer.
